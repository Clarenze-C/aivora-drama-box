- Acceptance evidence:
  - only valid transitions: full season (36 pairs), episode, provider-operation (8×8) and job
    matrices equal the frozen tables (checked by identity); terminal outcomes never change;
    recoverable outcomes hold progress until cleared with evidence;
  - crash/replay: a crashed job recovers and completes exactly once; a recovered job replays its
    committed step without repeating the effect; expired leases requeue and a zombie worker cannot
    finish; concurrent idempotent executions run the action once; the outbox is at-least-once and
    `consume_once` admits exactly one winner under races; 8 threads never share a claimed job;
  - worker/model principals cannot write human approvals (every non-human kind refused, nothing
    written); service credentials cannot act as humans and vice versa; CSRF required on unsafe
    methods; bad credentials are indistinguishable; sessions expire and revoke;
  - typed uncertainty never becomes success: an expired lease with an in-flight provider
    operation goes to NEEDS_RECONCILIATION; only explicit, evidenced reconciliation resolves it;
    UNKNOWN is never resubmitted; only proven NOT_FOUND can be;
  - AT-02 foundations (fixtures): workers/models refused; replayed final approval refused;
    material media change needs the same Gate 2 again; human without a decision cannot release.
- External effects: none (ephemeral loopback PostgreSQL, network guard active).
- Accepted limits (documented for later lanes): outbox dispatch is head-of-line and publishes
  inside its transaction (transport owner needs a poison/DLQ policy); WAITING_EXTERNAL polling is
  bounded only by provider-operation deadlines; `cancel()` leaves an in-flight provider operation
  for reconciliation and budget settlement; `lease_guard` is a worker discipline; approvals are
  judged by the database server clock; no login rate limiting (review lane sets cookie flags);
  idempotency records are never deleted (retention decision open).
