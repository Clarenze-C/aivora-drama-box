- Review round (sent back, fixed in the lane):
  - F-1 (confirmed by an integrator probe): a late ACCRUAL on an EXPIRED reservation was added
    on top of the assumed worst case (700,000 hold, 500,000 real usage counted as 1,000,000),
    which near the cap would record a false, permanent overrun. Now an expired reservation
    counts `max(reserved, real usage)`; only the delta moves, with an in-transaction invariant
    check. Tests cover within/above the worst case, ADJUSTMENT and a near-cap no-false-overrun
    case; a mutation reinstating the old logic fails four tests.
  - F-2: release/expiry/settlement reasons were not persisted. They are now stored in
    `closed_reason`; a RELEASED reservation keeps its state and reason (DB-enforced).
- M1 independent validation (round 1) reopened the task (three CRITICAL, two MAJOR, three MINOR
  findings reproduced by probes); fixed in the lane, with guardian contract 1.3.0:
  - V-1: shutdown/cleanup privilege only for production GPU/recovery/storage (contract + one
    service helper for both the reserve floor and the blocked-account bypass);
  - V-2: `release()` is refused while a provider operation on the reservation is in flight;
  - V-3: the worst case must cover the units the ceilings allow for the price unit (tokens,
    media seconds, GPU seconds); a missing ceiling is uncertain exposure; tested per PriceUnit;
  - V-4: research attribution that would break cap − shutdown reserve or the RESEARCH allocation
    is refused atomically (residue named, stays on the research account);
  - V-5: SETTLEMENT/ACCRUAL on a closed reservation is refused; ADJUSTMENT is the late-billing path;
  - V-12/V-13/V-17: remaining excludes the shutdown reserve (shown separately); the invariant
    checker derives each reservation's settled amount from its charges; expiry follows only the
    injected clock. Mutation checks confirm the V-1 and V-4 tests fail without the fixes.
  Lane tests after the fix: 160 passed.
- Acceptance evidence:
  - AT-01 under real concurrency: 16 barrier-released threads × 700,000 against the GPU
    allocation plus spill; exactly 6 succeed in each of three rounds, the rest get
    `BudgetExhausted`, totals equal the reservation rows, and the boundary is exact
    (550,000 fits, 550,001 does not). A mutation removing the account `FOR UPDATE` makes the race
    tests fail. A mixed-category storm never overspends.
  - no double counting: one reservation per semantic key under 16 racing threads; replayed
    receipts counted once; credits recorded but never restore allowance; failed jobs' spend is
    kept; settlements racing new reservations keep every total exact.
  - exact integer micro-USD everywhere (SUMs cast to bigint); protected reserves and the
    shutdown floor hold; only production may spill from recovery headroom; metered management
    can never reach render or shutdown funds; GPU intervals counted once (EXCLUDE constraint +
    pre-check, also across two episodes racing one warm pod).
  - unknown price, non-USD price/receipt, zero exposure and fake prices outside OFFLINE_MOCK block
    paid dispatch; measured receipts are refused in OFFLINE_MOCK and fakes outside it.
  - observed overruns are recorded with the profile, block further paid work, and are never
    erased by later settlements; the cost view lists overruns and unsettled/expired exposure.
  - `assert_consistent` recomputes every account/category counter from reservations, charges,
    allocations and overruns at the end of nearly every scenario.
- External effects: none (ephemeral loopback PostgreSQL, network guard active).
