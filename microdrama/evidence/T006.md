    the `DisclosureKind` decode instead of a typed SchemaInvalid.
  - V-36 (MINOR): `PresetRegistry.compatible([])` returns every enabled preset (fail-open) where
    `CapabilityRegistry.evaluate` refuses an empty requirement as a caller bug.
