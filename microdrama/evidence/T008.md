- Acceptance evidence:
  - authenticated owner actions only: worker, model, scheduler and webhook principals are refused
    for both gates at service and HTTP level (403, nothing written); unauthenticated → 401 (JSON),
    303 to login (pages); CSRF required on every unsafe form/JSON post; logout revokes the session;
  - stale or hash-mismatched packages cannot approve: package hash, media hashes (compared as a
    set), management-policy hash, release-scope hash and revision each → 409 with nothing written;
    a command bound to package v1 is stale once v2 exists;
  - Gate 2 enabled only when `gate2_blockers` is empty: open CRITICAL/BLOCKED findings, rights or
    capability blockers, tampered/unpersisted assets, live or unverified-stopped GPU leases,
    estimated captions, a policy hash differing from the approved scope, a non-current or
    non-READY package, or an episode outside AWAITING_FINAL_REVIEW → 409 GateNotReady; the page
    still shows the preview and blockers but no Approve form;
  - replay idempotent, including two racing threads (one approval, one job);
  - no auto-approval timer: days of clock advance leave the episode waiting; a contract test
    forbids timers/schedulers in the module;
  - material media/caption/overlay/cover/hook changes are material; bounded title/caption/
    description/hashtag/reply edits inside the approved policy are not;
  - REVISE records timecoded feedback, moves the episode to the most upstream stage its feedback
    needs, increments `qa_cycle`, enqueues `qa.plan_repairs`, and the revised episode returns to
    the same gate with a new package and can be approved there;
  - identity-lock hook: episode one runs it inside the approval transaction (a raising hook rolls
    the whole approval back), a missing hook refuses episode one (409, no bypass), later episodes
    do not need it;
  - review view lists UNKNOWN coverage explicitly and shows no aggregate score; media streams only
    to an owner session with private headers and byte ranges, never via a public URL; costs show
    paid headroom and the shutdown reserve separately.
- External effects: none (in-process TestClient, private filesystem store, network guard active).
