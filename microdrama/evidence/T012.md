- Acceptance evidence (task_breakdown §5 T012 — every required fault drill has a test):
  - **ambiguous create**: timeout-after-create adopts the existing pod by client token
    (never a second create); timeout-without-create retries once with the same token;
    persistently ambiguous stays marked for reconciliation
    (`test_ambiguous_create_*`, `test_persistently_ambiguous_create_*`);
  - **unavailable GPU / blocked hardware**: hardware outside the allowlist, GPUs outside
    the allowlist and non-USD or missing prices block the claim before any budget money
    moves; an exhausted account refuses the claim with no pod (`test_*_blocks_*`,
    `test_budget_stop_exhausted_account_refuses_claim_without_any_pod`);
  - **dead worker / expired heartbeat**: janitor sweeps a stale-heartbeat lease to
    ORPHANED, stops the pod and settles the hold from durable state alone;
    claim-without-pod gets the unbound-hold grace (`test_crash_after_create_*`,
    heartbeat/expiry boundary tests);
  - **budget stop**: with the account drained to exactly the cap (rule-true per-category
    holds; the recovery tranche is a `pod.stop` cleanup reserve), shutdown still stops and
    verifies the pod, records `pod_lease.cleanup_grant_refused`, settles the real usage,
    and never breaches the cap or sets the blocked flag
    (`test_budget_stop_cannot_reserve_a_shutdown_grant_but_the_pod_still_stops`);
  - **shutdown failure**: stop+terminate both failing leaves durable RELEASING state with
    a `pod_lease.stop_failed` event; the independent janitor finishes it to ORPHANED,
    STOPPED and SETTLED without the failed process (`test_shutdown_failure_*`);
    a transient stop failure retries within the shutdown; persistent failure escalates to
    terminate; shutdown replay is idempotent (one provider stop, replayed result);
  - **no orphan/duplicate acquisition**: concurrent claims of one key produce exactly one
    lease; the pilot single-live-lease rule holds across episodes and warm-pod transfers
    (each paid interval owned and billed exactly once — exclusion constraint plus one
    transaction);
  - **cannot stop unrelated resources**: foreign pods are invisible to the lifecycle and
    untouched (`foreign_touches == []`); the janitor scans only its exact owned tag;
  - **no GPU left waiting on a human**: the human-wait transition guard refuses while a
    lease is live; `stop_for_human_wait` performs the full verified shutdown first; a
    released lease cannot be resurrected once the gate is open (heartbeat → PolicyDenied);
    the janitor also sweeps live leases of episodes already behind a human gate;
  - **real resources are T031 only**: `RunpodLiveAdapter` exists but resolves to nothing
    without configured credentials; the synthetic mock price is refused by the budget
    service outside OFFLINE_MOCK (`test_mock_price_matches_the_fake_pod_billing` pins
    fake price = fake billing).
