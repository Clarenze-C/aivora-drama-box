- External effects: none. OFFLINE_MOCK; ffmpeg/ffprobe are local binaries on tmp
  paths only; no network, no paid provider, nothing committed.
- Byte-stability, honestly: exports are byte-identical for identical inputs on ONE
  ffmpeg build with `-threads 1`, `+bitexact` flags and `-map_metadata -1` (test
  hashes two independent runs). They are NOT byte-stable across ffmpeg versions,
  across hosts, or if multi-threaded/hardware encoders are ever enabled — and MP4
  mux timestamps only stay identical for the same build. The EDL, caption tracks
  and SRTs are canonical-JSON/text deterministic everywhere.
