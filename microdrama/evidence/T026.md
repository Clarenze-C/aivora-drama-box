## Acceptance evidence (task_breakdown §5 T026 row)

- **Free CTA never resolves to an unlabeled paywall**: a PUBLIC_FREE unlock
  carries ONLY the verified public URL and never the member paywall link
  (`test_public_free_unlock_carries_the_public_url_and_never_the_paywall`); the
  catalog side refuses a PUBLIC_FREE entry whose URL does not match a
  `PUBLISHED_VERIFIED` platform post of that episode
  (`test_public_free_requires_a_published_verified_post_url`), so an unlisted or
  promised link can never back a "free now" claim. The paid offer is always

  checkout (`test_not_entitled_member_gets_price_plus_free_date_before_checkout`:
  `$3.00/month` + the free date in the same message).

  verifies the caller's `approval_event_id` against a real Gate 2
  (`EPISODE_FINAL`/APPROVE/this-episode) `approval_events` row — a Gate 1 season
  approval, a random id, or no approval is refused
  (`test_early_access_needs_a_real_gate2_approval_for_the_episode`); a
  zero/negative price is not a truthful recurring price
  (`test_zero_price_is_not_a_truthful_recurring_price`); the provider accepts
  only `EARLY_ACCESS_MEMBERS` entries (fake + adapter both enforce).

## Implementation limits and honest unknowns

- **No live transport exists.** Everything is OFFLINE_MOCK over the T007
  `FakeMembershipProvider`/`FakeCommunityProvider`; all results are
  `Provenance.FAKE_SYNTHETIC`, all URLs `*.invalid`. The provider call in
  `publish_early_access` happens between two transactions (offline fixture); a
  live T032 route must move it behind the outbox/provider-operation machinery
  before any resubmission — noted for the integrator.
- **Webhook payload shape is the documented fixture contract**
  (`{"id", "type": "members:pledge:create"|"members:pledge:delete", "member_ref",
  "episode_id"}`); the live Patreon payload mapping (v3 webhook envelope, tier
  ids) is a T032 concern. Raw `member_ref` values are hashed
  (`member_ref_hash`) BEFORE any persistence — including inside the stored
  `webhook_events.payload` (`_sanitized_payload`), which keeps audit data while
  honouring "never persist raw member identifiers".
- **Entitlement checks and DM sends are metered as one REQUEST each** at the
  synthetic T007 price (1000 micro-USD) through the real BudgetService with
  COMMUNITY-queue ownership and no spill from protected categories; a live
  adapter must supply a real provider quote (unknown price blocks dispatch by
  policy). A transiently DEFERRED send keeps its reservation and retries on the
  next inbound event with the same dedupe key and reservation
  (`test_deferred_send_keeps_its_reservation_and_retries_cleanly`).
- The DM lane sends at most ONE unlock message per
  (platform, account, author, episode) plus the policy's per-conversation cap;
  it classifies its events with `classification='membership_keyword_dm'` so the
  T025 organic lane can distinguish its own reply traffic on the shared tables.
- The catalog has no free-text field, so owner decisions (free-date change,
  withdrawal) are recorded as append-only `domain_events`
  (`membership.free_date_changed`, `membership.withdrawn`,
  `membership.early_access_opened`, `membership.public_free`) rather than new
  contract fields — no contract change required.
