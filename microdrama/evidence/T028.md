## Acceptance evidence (task_breakdown §5 T028 row)

- **AT-08 flood cannot starve due releases**: with 250 community jobs queued
  ahead of a due `publish.poll_status` job, one `run_cycle` executes the
  release AND serves the community reservation (4 slots) — every cycle visits
  every reserved queue, so a community backlog can never push a due release
  behind it
  (`test_due_release_completes_in_the_first_cycle_under_flood`);
  production work progresses the same way under the same flood
  (`test_production_work_also_progresses_under_flood`).
- **Community cannot spend protected funds**: proven from behavior — a
  community-queue reservation of GPU_GENERATION is refused by the budget
  service's own ownership gate before anything is held
  (`test_community_queue_may_not_reserve_protected_categories`, message
  "queue community may not reserve GPU_GENERATION"), and draining the whole
  flood moves no money at all (zero reservations, zero charges —
  `test_flood_processing_moves_no_money_at_all`).
- **Retries do not bypass the limiter**: a denied bound job finishes
  RETRY_WAIT with the limiter's own ``retry_after_ms`` and its handler never
  ran (`test_denial_defers_without_running_the_handler`,
  `test_retry_after_is_the_limiters_own_wait`); the retry re-acquires from
  scratch and can defer again
  (`test_a_retry_reacquires_and_can_defer_again`). Rate-deferrals consume
  attempts by frozen job-service semantics (each re-claim of a QUEUED job
  increments ``attempt``); that truth is pinned visibly — a starved bucket
  fails the job after ``max_attempts`` with error_code ending RATE_LIMITED,
  never a silent drop (`test_rate_deferrals_consume_attempts_by_frozen_semantics`).
  Deployments must size ``max_attempts`` for rate waits; recorded here as the
  operational rule.
- **Shared provider quota across queues**: a publication job and a community
  job bound to one provider-account key take turns through the same bucket —
  one token, one EXECUTED + one RATE_DEFERRED per cycle
  (`test_shared_key_serializes_across_queues`).
- **Outages resume durable jobs without duplicate effects**: a worker that
  crashes after the effect but before finishing leaves the lease to expire;
  the janitor requeues the job; the retry replays idempotently and the
  durable effect exists exactly once
  (`test_crashed_job_resumes_with_one_durable_effect`). The runtime never
  re-runs ambiguous provider work itself: an expired lease with an in-flight
  provider operation lands in NEEDS_RECONCILIATION (frozen semantics, pinned
  in `test_quarantined_jobs_are_listed_not_requeued`) and the janitor only
  surfaces it.
- **Initial one-episode GPU ownership enforced**: while a lease-held
  ``render.shot`` for an episode is RUNNING, a second render of the SAME
  episode defers with GPU_EPISODE_BUSY (`test_second_render_of_the_same_episode_defers`);
  a different episode renders freely
  (`test_a_different_episode_renders_freely`); non-GPU jobs of the busy
  episode are not blocked
  (`test_non_gpu_jobs_of_the_same_episode_are_not_blocked`); finishing a
  render releases the episode (`test_finished_renders_release_the_episode`).
  The rendering lane's pod-lease ownership remains the paid-side backstop
  underneath.
- **Distinct routing / reserved concurrency / durable jobs**: job routing is
  the frozen spec table (contract lane pins every job type routes to exactly
  one of the three queues and all three queues carry work); a deployment's
  ``QueueSlots`` reservations cap each queue per cycle
  (`test_slots_cap_each_queue_per_cycle`); a zero-slot declaration is refused
  as a configuration mistake; jobs live in the durable ``jobs`` table with the
  queue CHECK pinned at the contract level.
- **Independently scheduled janitor**: ``python -m microdrama.workers.janitor``
  needs only the database, the budget service and configuration — never a
  worker process or queue. One sweep requeues expired leases
  (`test_sweep_requeues_an_expired_lease`), expires stale budget holds, and
  reports queue depths + the quarantine population, appending a
  ``workers.janitor.swept`` evidence event
  (`test_sweep_reports_depths_and_appends_evidence`); a failing step is
  recorded without blocking the rest
  (`test_a_step_failure_does_not_block_the_rest`).

## Lane decisions recorded for the integrator (T029) and later waves

1. **Capability pinning (open item from the T025/T026 wave) — DECIDED: defer
   to T032.** ``CommunityProvider`` has no capability surface; the runtime does
   not invent one. Community admission stays exactly what the frozen specs and
   the community service's own gates allow; real platform COMMENT_* capability
   pinning lands with T032's account qualification, where capability evidence
   exists. (Documented in `runtime.py`'s module docstring so it is findable.)
2. **Limiter state is process-local by design.** The real ``SharedRateLimiter``
   is the deterministic token bucket over the frozen protocol; its decisions
   are pinned identical to the T007 fake oracle (contract lane). Durable
   cross-process bucket state (Redis via ``settings.redis_url``) is deployment
   configuration for T029 — the protocol is the contract, and no schema change
   is required for it.
3. **Rate-wait attempt accounting** (above): deployments size ``max_attempts``
   per job kind so a legitimate rate wait cannot exhaust a healthy job; the
   failure mode when they do not is a visible FAILED, never a bypass.
4. **Handlers are a registry, not a rewrite**: T027's ``analytics_handlers``
   and every lane's job entry points plug into ``WorkerDeployment.handlers``;
   the runtime adds no business logic of its own.
