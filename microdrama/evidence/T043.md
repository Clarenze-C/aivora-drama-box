# T043 — poster, preview and creative lineage

Contract package 2.10.0. Local derivative creation only; no provider or
public action.

The editing service creates a static poster and optional episode-one preview
from accepted, exact-version assets. Video and separate audio inputs retain
their full lineage, hashes, source ranges and per-source current rights. The
preview is capped at 90 seconds; its reveal cutoff is in footage source time,
independent of output timeline. Posters bind approved title/CTA text boxes,
platform safe zones and readability evidence. Both derivatives carry a
derivation-manifest hash and no release obligation. Changed sources supersede
only dependent derivatives. The existing three short hook candidates remain
separate.

ReleasePackage includes typed creative derivatives in its Gate 2 package hash
when present; EpisodeReviewView exposes them through that package. The local
composed fake test renders and persists poster/preview outputs, checks video
and audio lineage, restart replay, package hash change, review projection and
targeted invalidation. T044 owns the eventual workbench presentation.
