### R1 — allocation basis (G1)

`BillingRepository.derive_allocations` now derives Runpod lease episodes from
`pod_leases` compute windows: the GPU/CPU denominator is the Pod's existence seconds
inside the bucket (chain root's `create_requested_at` to the last lease's verified
stop/terminate; bucket end while unknown — conservative), never the bucket length.
Existence covered by no lease goes to the root lease's episode (recovery pool when the
Pod has no root lease); with no root, existence is measured from the earliest lease
window (interpretation noted below). A non-zero bucket without recorded existence is
100% recovery. Disk keeps storage-ownership weights; existence the ownership rows do
not cover is recovery. The ownership query's sixth bind was `start` where the bucket
end was meant — open ownership rows (valid_until NULL) were silently excluded; the
binding is fixed now that rows are actually written. The fake provider path
(`pod_usage_intervals`) is unchanged.

### R5 + R6 — period evidence and idempotent replay (G5/G6)

`verify_invoice_period` is the one public verification API (pod-scoped; account-wide
scope dropped). Preconditions, in order: a completed `BUCKETS`/`EMPTY` query covering
the period with `observed_at >= period_end + settle_lag`; no skipped-open buckets
inside the period; period edges on bucket boundaries. MATCHED writes the period row
and, in the same transaction, an internal revision N+1 `INVOICE_VERIFIED` bucket (same
raw hash — the widened 0025 key), a MATCHED per-bucket verification, the budget
finalization row, and zero-delta allocations that post nothing. MATCHED_ZERO covers a
failed create against an `EMPTY` query. MISMATCH writes only the period row.
Replay key `(account, pod, period, evidence_sha256)`: identical replay is a no-op
returning the stored outcome; a differing amount/currency raises `IdempotencyConflict`;
new evidence for a verified period raises `Conflict` unless an audited supersede
(`supersedes_evidence_id` + actor + reason) names the row it replaces; integrity
violations surface as typed errors, never raw `IntegrityError`. Spend is never
lowered: a lower invoice is a mismatch and nothing is decremented.

### R7 — lease billing finality and the BILLED settlement (G5/G9/G16)

`LeaseBillingReadiness` (five states + `pending_since`) replaces `accounting_readiness`
(removed together with `BillingAccountingReadiness`). Coverage is by overlap over the
lease's compute window plus any storage window its episode owns on the Pod up to
`terminate_verified_at`, with no gap, against MATCHED/MATCHED_ZERO period evidence;
DISPUTED covering buckets or an open (unsuperseded) MISMATCHED period row give
DISPUTED; unresolved recovery gives RECOVERY_REQUIRED; missing-but-queried time gives
AWAITING_INVOICE, unqueried time PENDING_PROVIDER_FINALITY. `settle_billed_lease`
posts the deterministic BILLED `SETTLEMENT` (amount = GPU ACCRUAL+ADJUSTMENT postings
on R, which may be 0; `observed_at` from the lease row so replays are idempotent);
the ledger releases exactly the unused remainder from ACTIVE or EXPIRED holds; any
posted-versus-invoice overstatement is appended as a `billing.overstatement_recorded`
event. REOPENED is derived: a settled lease with a covering revision newer than its
settlement reports `reopened=True` (the revision's positive delta posts an ADJUSTMENT
on the closed reservation).

### SPEC-GAP / interpretation notes

- No `SPEC-GAP` against money semantics was needed; no guardian contract change was
  required (examples' fixture values only, artifacts regenerated).
- Interpretation (R1): with no root lease on the Pod, existence is measured from the
  earliest lease window start — the spec fixes the root's create request as the start
  but does not define the no-root start; uncovered time still goes to recovery in
  that case, which is the conservative direction.
- Interpretation (R7): the invoice sum used for overstatement evidence is the maximum
  amount over the (non-superseded) MATCHED/MATCHED_ZERO periods covering the lease
  window, so a superseded higher invoice can never mask an overstatement.
- The R4 settle lag (15 minutes) is an unverified assumption about Runpod bucket
  settlement, configurable per `BillingRepository` and recorded as such in the
  runbook.

### Defect the composed canary exposed (red → green in T045-A code)

`BillingRepository.derive_allocations` truncated sub-second existence/ownership to
`int(...)` seconds, so a canary that lived 0.05 s produced a zero-weight disk owner
row: the whole DISK component allocated as a zero delta and
